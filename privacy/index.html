Privacy policy

Version 3.1 · in effect from 2026-11-18

In short

ANVL Coaching stores what you need to train and, if you have a coach, what your coach needs to coach you: your profile, the workouts you run and the sets you log, your goals and reviews, and — only if you choose — your intake answers, body entries and progress photos. You see it; a coach you have joined sees it while they coach you; nobody else does, apart from the people who run the service and the providers that host it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we are

Georges Joseph Maalouf, Zahle, Bekaa, Lebanon, operates ANVL Coaching and is the controller of your personal data: the person who decides how and why it is used. You can write to us about anything in this policy at support@anvlathletics.com, wherever you live, including in the European Union and the United Kingdom. A coach you join is a separate, independent controller of the data they use to coach you (see Coaches).

What we collect

Account and profile, from Google or Apple and from you: your name and email address, and your Google profile picture if you sign in with Google; your phone number, gender, training experience and sessions per week, if you enter them; your language, time zone and the day your week starts; whether you coach, your coach code, and which coach, if any, you have joined.

Sign in with Apple: if you sign in with Apple, Apple gives us your name the first time only, and an email address. You can choose to hide your email. Apple then gives us a private relay address that forwards to you, and we never see your real one. After you sign in, our server also receives a token from Apple that is tied to your Apple sign-in. We keep it on our servers for one purpose only: to ask Apple to remove the app’s access when your account is deleted. The app and other people cannot read it.

Coach profile: if you coach, your name, photo and headline are shown to the athletes you invite, to anyone who enters your code, and on the invitation images you share. The photo you upload is stored at a public address, so anyone who has the address can open it.

Intake questionnaire: if a coach asks you to complete it, your answers about your training history, goals, health conditions and injuries, including the health flags your coach sees. Health conditions and injuries are health data, and you choose what to write.

Training: the programmes and workouts you run, whether a coach planned them or you did; every set you log, with its load, reps and notes; when each session started and finished; exercise swaps and their reasons; personal records; goals; block reviews; the exercises and clips you create; and the coach’s notes, including those marked coach-only.

Body tracking, only if you turn it on: weight and measurement entries and their notes, progress photos, your height and starting weight, and bodyweight goals. This is health data.

Relationships: the invitations you send or receive, including the email address a coach invites; the requests you make; when each coaching relationship started and ended; and whom you have blocked.

Reports: if you report someone, whom and what you reported, your reason and note, and what we did. If someone reports you, the same information about you.

Agreements: which versions of the terms and this policy you accepted, the consents you gave or withdrew (body tracking and, if you coach, the coaching duties), and when.

Purchases: which plan you bought, when it renews and the store’s purchase identifier. The store you bought from handles your payment details: Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone. We never see them.

Preferences: units, workout layout, tempo audio, rest alerts and the notifications you want.

Technical: the app version; if you turn notifications on, a device token so that we can deliver them; and, if you installed the app on Android from a coach’s invitation link, the invitation code Google Play passes to the app. Our hosting provider keeps routine server logs, including IP addresses, for a short time for security and troubleshooting. The app contains no advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools.

Camera, photos and microphone: the app uses the camera when you take a progress photo or a photo to share, record an exercise clip, or scan an invitation QR code, which is read and not stored; it opens your photo library only when you pick a photo or clip; and it uses the microphone only when a clip is recorded with sound. A photo you share stays on your phone: the image with your session’s numbers is made on the device and goes only where you send it.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide your account, your training and the coaching relationships you choose. This is necessary to perform our contract with you: the terms.
  • Body tracking data, only with your explicit consent. You give it in the app and can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy and data; withdrawing deletes that data.
  • The health conditions and injuries you describe in your intake, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by entering them for your coach. You can correct them, or ask us to delete them, at any time.
  • To keep the service safe — reviewing reports, enforcing the terms and preventing abuse — and to keep a record of what we did. This is our legitimate interest in a service people can trust.
  • To keep a record of the agreements and consents you give, and to answer requests about your data. These are our legal obligations.
  • Your purchase history, to give you the plan you paid for and to keep the records the law requires. This is necessary to perform our contract with you, and a legal obligation.
  • The Apple token, to remove the app’s access to your Apple Account when your account is deleted. This is part of providing your account and deleting it properly, under our contract with you.
  • To send the notifications you switch on, and only those.

We use your data for nothing else. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who can see it

  • You: everything you have logged.
  • A coach you have joined, while they coach you: your profile, intake answers, sessions, records and analytics from your first day on the app, your goals and reviews, and, while body tracking is on, your body entries and progress photos. Notes a coach marks coach-only stay with the coach. When the coaching ends, the coach keeps a read-only archive of the sessions logged while they coached you: your name and training record only, never body data, photos, intake answers, session notes or swap reasons.
  • A coach you ask to coach you: your name, your training basics, how much you have logged and the note you send, so that they can decide. Everything else opens only if they accept.
  • Other athletes and other coaches: nothing. There are no public profiles and no leaderboards. A coach’s name, photo and headline are the only things shown to people they do not coach.
  • The people who run the service: platform administrators can access data to support you, to review a report and to keep the service running.
  • The service providers listed below, only to host and deliver the service, and public authorities, only when the law requires it.

Coaches

A coach uses your data for their own coaching, under the duties they accept in the terms (If you coach): only to coach you, confidentially, and no longer than the coaching lasts. A coach outside the European Union and the United Kingdom who coaches you there is bound by the standard contractual clauses for that access. You can ask a coach, or us, for what they have written about you. A coach keeps the read-only archive of your sessions on their legitimate interest in a record of their services. You are told when the coaching starts and when it ends. You can object to the archive when you leave, or later by writing to support@anvlathletics.com.

Service providers

  • Supabase Pte. Ltd hosts the database, file storage and sign-in sessions in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1), encrypted at rest and in transit, under a data processing agreement that includes the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum.
  • Google provides sign-in. We receive your name, email address and profile picture; we never see your password.
  • Apple provides sign-in on iPhone. We receive your name the first time, and your email address or a private relay address. We never see your Apple password. Apple knows that you use the app, under Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • Expo delivers app updates and, if you turn notifications on, sends push notifications to your device token.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries push notifications to Android phones, and the Apple Push Notification service to iPhones.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) manages subscriptions on our behalf. It receives the store’s purchase identifier, an opaque account id, your device type and when the app was last used, under a data processing agreement with the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum. Its sub-processors are in the United States, and it lists them in Annex 3 of that agreement at https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa/.
  • Google Play is the seller of record for purchases made on Android and handles payment under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • The App Store is the seller of record for purchases made on iPhone, and Apple handles payment under Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • MuscleWiki content — exercise names, descriptions and demonstration videos — is copied to our own storage. No personal data is sent to MuscleWiki.
  • Instagram and the other apps you share to receive a shared image only when you choose to send it to them, under their own policies.

Photos and clips

Progress photos are stored privately and are shown only through short-lived links, to you and to your coach. They are never public. To remove a photo, delete its entry in the app or write to support@anvlathletics.com.

Exercise clips uploaded since this version are stored privately and played through short-lived links to the people who can see that exercise; clips uploaded before it remain at unlisted public addresses. Exercise thumbnails and coach profile photos are stored at public addresses. Each account has a storage limit for clips, shown in the app.

Reports and suspension

When you report something, we keep the report, a copy of the content as it was when you reported it, and what we did about it, until the account that filed it or the account it concerns is deleted, so that we can show what happened if asked. We do not tell the person reported who reported them.

When we hide content, it disappears from the app and any files it held are deleted; we keep its text with the report so that the decision can be reviewed or reversed. When we suspend an account, it is signed out on every device, its notification tokens are deleted, and it cannot sign in until we lift the suspension.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists, because your history is what makes trends, records and reviews work. Body tracking data is deleted when you withdraw your consent, and reports are kept as described above. If you signed in with Apple, the token is kept while your account exists. It is revoked at Apple and deleted when the account is deleted. When you ask us to delete your account, we lock it within 10 days of your request and keep it for 30 days, during which it can be restored. We then erase or anonymise your data, and it leaves our backups on their normal rotation after that. A programme a coach wrote is the coach’s work, not your personal data, and stays with the coach. If coaching ends while you keep your account, the coach’s read-only archive of your sessions is kept for 6 years after the coaching ends, then deleted. It holds no body data, photos, intake answers, session notes or swap reasons. It is deleted sooner with your account, and the coach keeps nothing of it after that. Purchase records are kept for 10 years after the subscription ends, even after your account is deleted, because the law requires us to keep business records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw a consent at any time without affecting what was done before. You can see and correct most of your data in the app. For anything else — a copy of your data (as JSON or CSV, including what a coach wrote about you), a correction you cannot make yourself, or the deletion of your account — write to support@anvlathletics.com from the address on your account. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, say so in your message. We will confirm who you are another way. We answer within 10 days, and we may ask you to confirm who you are.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please write to us first. You also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority where you live or work: in the European Union, your national supervisory authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm: the request reaches us from your account. You can also write to support@anvlathletics.com yourself, or follow the steps at https://mofftea26.github.io/anvl-legal/delete-account/.

If you have a subscription, cancel it first in the store you bought it from: Google Play on Android, or your Apple Account settings on iPhone. Deleting your account does not cancel it. When the account is deleted, we also delete your record at RevenueCat. We keep your purchase records for the period set out in How long we keep it.

If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to remove the app’s access to your Apple Account when the account is deleted, and the token we kept is deleted with it. If Apple still lists ANVL Coaching afterwards, you can remove it yourself in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name and Sign in with Apple.

Within 10 days of your request we lock the account: you cannot sign in and nobody can see it. We keep it for 30 days, during which it can be restored if you ask. Then we delete the account and everything in it: your profile and sign-in, intake answers, the programmes assigned to you, sessions and sets, body entries and progress photos, goals, reviews, notes, consents, device tokens, the reports you filed or that concern you, and your links with coaches. A coach’s account takes the coach’s own programmes, workouts and custom exercises with it. Copies leave our backups on their normal rotation. We confirm by reply when it is done.

On your phone

So that you can train without a connection, the app keeps a copy of your plan — your calendar, workouts, exercises and session history — and the workout in progress, including sets not yet sent, in its own storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Your sign-in session is encrypted with a key held in the phone’s secure keystore. Exercise videos and images the app has loaded may also be cached on the phone. The copy of your plan is removed when you sign out; a workout or sets not yet sent stay on the phone under your account until you sign back in and they reach us, so that a sign-out never loses a session.

Security

Every row in the database is protected by access rules that decide who can read and change it, and the app cannot read what those rules do not allow. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our hosting provider, sign-in is handled by Google or Apple, and the keys that reach third-party services are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn that we have, we delete the account.

International transfers

We operate from Lebanon, and your data is stored in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1). Our providers may process it in other countries; where data from the European Union or the United Kingdom goes to a country without an adequacy decision, they rely on the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum. A coach you join sees your data from wherever they are; a coach outside the European Union and the United Kingdom who coaches you there is bound by the standard contractual clauses that form part of the terms. RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase data in the United States. Apple processes sign-in and purchase data in the United States and in the other countries where it operates, under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

The version and the date at the top show which policy applies. Before a material change takes effect, we tell you in the app and ask you to accept the new policy before you continue.
