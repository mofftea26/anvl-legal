Privacy policy

Version 1.1 · in effect from 2026-09-18

In short

ANVL Coaching stores what you and your coach need to train together: your profile, your intake answers, the workouts you are assigned and the sets you log, your body entries and progress photos, your goals and reviews. Your coach can see it; nobody else can, apart from the people who run the service and the providers that host it. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

Account and profile, from Google and from you: your name, email address and profile photo from Google sign-in; your phone number, gender, height, starting weight, training experience and sessions per week if you enter them; whether you are a coach or an athlete, and which coach you belong to.

Intake questionnaire: your answers about training history, goals, health conditions and injuries, including the health flags your coach sees. This is health-related information, and you choose what to write.

Training: the programmes and workouts assigned to you; every set you log with its load, reps, effort and notes; when a session started and finished and which layout you used; personal records; goals; block reviews; the coach’s notes, including the ones marked coach-only.

Body: weight and measurement entries, the notes on them, and progress photos.

Preferences: units, runner layout, tempo audio, rest-timer behaviour and which notifications you want.

Technical: the app version, and, if you turn notifications on, a device token so we can deliver them. The app does not use advertising identifiers or analytics trackers.

Camera and microphone: the app asks for the camera when you take a progress photo or a photo to share, and, for coaches, when recording an exercise clip; it asks for the microphone only when a clip is recorded with sound. A photo you share stays on your phone: the image with your session’s numbers is made on the device and goes only where you send it.

Why we use it

  • To run the coaching relationship: your coach plans, prescribes and reviews from what you log.
  • To show you your own history, trends, records and goals.
  • To send the notifications you switch on, and only those.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to help you when you write to us.

We use the data for nothing else. There is no profiling, no advertising and no sale of data.

Who can see it

  • You: everything you have logged and everything your coach has chosen to share with you.
  • Your coach: your profile, intake, sessions, body entries, photos, goals and reviews. Notes a coach marks coach-only stay with the coach.
  • Other athletes: nothing. There are no public profiles and no leaderboards.
  • The people who run the service: platform administrators can access data to support you and to keep the service running.
  • Service providers listed below, only to host and deliver the service.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in sessions. Your data is stored in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1), encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Google provides sign-in. We receive your name, email address and profile photo; we never see your password.
  • Expo delivers app updates and, when you turn notifications on, push notifications through your device token.
  • MuscleWiki content, the exercise names, descriptions and demonstration videos, is mirrored on our own storage. No personal data is sent to MuscleWiki.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries push notifications to Android phones.
  • Instagram and the other apps you share to receive a shared image only when you choose to send it to them, under their own policies.
  • Exercise clips a coach uploads live in that coach’s exercise library and are served through unlisted links to the coach and the coach’s athletes.

Progress photos

Photos are stored in a private bucket and are shown only through short-lived links to you and your coach. They are never public. To have a photo removed, delete the entry in the app or email support@anvlathletics.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active, because your history is what makes trends, records and reviews work. When your account is deleted we erase or anonymise your data within 30 days, and it leaves our backups on their normal rotation after that. A coach’s copy of a programme template is not your personal data and is kept.

Your rights

You can see and correct most of your data in the app. For a copy of your data, a correction you cannot make yourself, or deletion of your account, email support@anvlathletics.com from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my account: it writes the request to support@anvlathletics.com for you, from the address on your account. You can also write to that address yourself, or follow the steps at https://mofftea26.github.io/anvl-legal/delete-account/.

Within 30 days we delete the account and everything in it: your profile and sign-in, intake answers, programmes assigned to you, sessions and sets, body entries and progress photos, goals, reviews, notes and notification settings. A coach’s account takes the coach’s own programmes, workouts and custom exercises with it. Copies leave our backups on their normal rotation. We confirm by reply when it is done.

On your phone

To keep a workout running without a connection, the app stores the current session and unsent sets on your phone in encrypted storage keyed by the phone’s secure keystore. They are sent when you are back online and removed when you sign out of that device.

Security

Every row in the database is protected by access rules that decide who can read and change it; the app cannot read what those rules do not allow. Traffic is encrypted, sign-in is delegated to Google, and secrets that talk to third-party services never live in the app.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over, unless a coach has agreed a younger athlete’s participation with a parent or guardian and local law allows it. We do not knowingly collect data from children outside that arrangement.

Where it goes

Your data is stored in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1) and may be accessed from wherever you, your coach and our providers are. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on our providers’ standard contractual protections.

Changes to this policy

The version and the date at the top tell you which policy applies. We will tell you in the app before a material change takes effect.
