Privacy policy

Version 2.0 · in effect from 2026-10-19

In short

ANVL Coaching stores what you need to train and, if you have a coach, what your coach needs to coach you: your profile, the workouts you run and the sets you log, your goals and reviews, and — only if you choose — your intake answers, body entries and progress photos. You see it; a coach you have joined sees it while they coach you; nobody else does, apart from the people who run the service and the providers that host it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we are

Georges Joseph Maalouf, Zahle, Bekaa, Lebanon, operates ANVL Coaching and is the controller of your personal data: the person who decides how and why it is used. You can write to us about anything in this policy at support@anvlathletics.com, wherever you live, including in the European Union and the United Kingdom. A coach you join is a separate, independent controller of the data they use to coach you (see Coaches).

What we collect

Account and profile, from Google and from you: your name, email address and Google profile picture; your phone number, gender, training experience and sessions per week, if you enter them; your language, time zone and the day your week starts; whether you coach, your coach code, and which coach, if any, you have joined.

Coach profile: if you coach, your name, photo and headline are shown to the athletes you invite, to anyone who enters your code, and on the invitation images you share. The photo you upload is stored at a public address, so anyone who has the address can open it.

Intake questionnaire: if a coach asks you to complete it, your answers about your training history, goals, health conditions and injuries, including the health flags your coach sees. Health conditions and injuries are health data, and you choose what to write.

Training: the programmes and workouts you run, whether a coach planned them or you did; every set you log, with its load, reps and notes; when each session started and finished; exercise swaps and their reasons; personal records; goals; block reviews; the exercises and clips you create; and the coach’s notes, including those marked coach-only.

Body tracking, only if you turn it on: weight and measurement entries and their notes, progress photos, your height and starting weight, and bodyweight goals. This is health data.

Relationships: the invitations you send or receive, including the email address a coach invites; the requests you make; when each coaching relationship started and ended; and whom you have blocked.

Reports: if you report someone, whom and what you reported, your reason and note, and what we did. If someone reports you, the same information about you.

Agreements: which versions of the terms and this policy you accepted, the consents you gave or withdrew (body tracking and, if you coach, the coaching duties), and when.

Preferences: units, workout layout, tempo audio, rest alerts and the notifications you want.

Technical: the app version; if you turn notifications on, a device token so that we can deliver them; and, if you installed the app on Android from a coach’s invitation link, the invitation code Google Play passes to the app. Our hosting provider keeps routine server logs, including IP addresses, for a short time for security and troubleshooting. The app contains no advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools.

Camera, photos and microphone: the app uses the camera when you take a progress photo or a photo to share, record an exercise clip, or scan an invitation QR code, which is read and not stored; it opens your photo library only when you pick a photo or clip; and it uses the microphone only when a clip is recorded with sound. A photo you share stays on your phone: the image with your session’s numbers is made on the device and goes only where you send it.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide your account, your training and the coaching relationships you choose. This is necessary to perform our contract with you: the terms.
  • Body tracking data, only with your explicit consent. You give it in the app and can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy and data; withdrawing deletes that data.
  • The health conditions and injuries you describe in your intake, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by entering them for your coach. You can correct them, or ask us to delete them, at any time.
  • To keep the service safe — reviewing reports, enforcing the terms and preventing abuse — and to keep a record of what we did. This is our legitimate interest in a service people can trust.
  • To keep a record of the agreements and consents you give, and to answer requests about your data. These are our legal obligations.
  • To send the notifications you switch on, and only those.

We use your data for nothing else. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who can see it

  • You: everything you have logged.
  • A coach you have joined, while they coach you: your profile, intake answers, sessions, records and analytics from your first day on the app, your goals and reviews, and, while body tracking is on, your body entries and progress photos. Notes a coach marks coach-only stay with the coach. When the coaching ends, the coach keeps a read-only archive of the sessions logged while they coached you: your name and training record only, never body data, photos or intake answers.
  • A coach you ask to coach you: your name, your training basics, how much you have logged and the note you send, so that they can decide. Everything else opens only if they accept.
  • Other athletes and other coaches: nothing. There are no public profiles and no leaderboards. A coach’s name, photo and headline are the only things shown to people they do not coach.
  • The people who run the service: platform administrators can access data to support you, to review a report and to keep the service running.
  • The service providers listed below, only to host and deliver the service, and public authorities, only when the law requires it.

Coaches

A coach uses your data for their own coaching, under the duties they accept in the terms (If you coach): only to coach you, confidentially, and no longer than the coaching lasts. A coach outside the European Union and the United Kingdom who coaches you there is bound by the standard contractual clauses for that access. You can ask a coach, or us, for what they have written about you.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage and sign-in sessions in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1), encrypted at rest and in transit, under a data processing agreement that includes the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum.
  • Google provides sign-in. We receive your name, email address and profile picture; we never see your password.
  • Expo delivers app updates and, if you turn notifications on, sends push notifications to your device token.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging carries push notifications to Android phones, and the Apple Push Notification service to iPhones.
  • MuscleWiki content — exercise names, descriptions and demonstration videos — is copied to our own storage. No personal data is sent to MuscleWiki.
  • Instagram and the other apps you share to receive a shared image only when you choose to send it to them, under their own policies.

Photos and clips

Progress photos are stored privately and are shown only through short-lived links, to you and to your coach. They are never public. To remove a photo, delete its entry in the app or write to support@anvlathletics.com.

Exercise clips uploaded since this version are stored privately and played through short-lived links to the people who can see that exercise; clips uploaded before it remain at unlisted public addresses. Exercise thumbnails and coach profile photos are stored at public addresses. Each account has a storage limit for clips, shown in the app.

Reports and suspension

When you report something, we keep the report, a copy of the content as it was when you reported it, and what we did about it, until the account that filed it or the account it concerns is deleted, so that we can show what happened if asked. We do not tell the person reported who reported them.

When we hide content, it disappears from the app and any files it held are deleted; we keep its text with the report so that the decision can be reviewed or reversed. When we suspend an account, it is signed out on every device, its notification tokens are deleted, and it cannot sign in until we lift the suspension.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists, because your history is what makes trends, records and reviews work. Body tracking data is deleted when you withdraw your consent, and reports are kept as described above. When your account is deleted, we erase or anonymise your data within 10 days, and it leaves our backups on their normal rotation after that. A programme a coach wrote is the coach’s work, not your personal data, and stays with the coach. If coaching ends while you keep your account, the coach’s read-only archive of your sessions remains while the coach’s account exists; it holds no body data, photos or intake answers, and it is deleted with your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw a consent at any time without affecting what was done before. You can see and correct most of your data in the app. For anything else — a copy of your data (as JSON or CSV, including what a coach wrote about you), a correction you cannot make yourself, or the deletion of your account — write to support@anvlathletics.com from the address on your account. We answer within 10 days, and we may ask you to confirm who you are.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please write to us first. You also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority where you live or work: in the European Union, your national supervisory authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my account: the app writes the request to support@anvlathletics.com for you, from the address on your account. You can also write to that address yourself, or follow the steps at https://mofftea26.github.io/anvl-legal/delete-account/.

Within 10 days we delete the account and everything in it: your profile and sign-in, intake answers, the programmes assigned to you, sessions and sets, body entries and progress photos, goals, reviews, notes, consents, device tokens, the reports you filed or that concern you, and your links with coaches. A coach’s account takes the coach’s own programmes, workouts and custom exercises with it. Copies leave our backups on their normal rotation. We confirm by reply when it is done.

On your phone

So that you can train without a connection, the app keeps a copy of your plan — your calendar, workouts, exercises and session history — and the workout in progress, including sets not yet sent, in its own storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Your sign-in session is encrypted with a key held in the phone’s secure keystore. Exercise videos and images the app has loaded may also be cached on the phone. The copy of your plan is removed when you sign out; a workout or sets not yet sent stay on the phone under your account until you sign back in and they reach us, so that a sign-out never loses a session.

Security

Every row in the database is protected by access rules that decide who can read and change it, and the app cannot read what those rules do not allow. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our hosting provider, sign-in is handled by Google, and the keys that reach third-party services are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn that we have, we delete the account.

International transfers

We operate from Lebanon, and your data is stored in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1). Our providers may process it in other countries; where data from the European Union or the United Kingdom goes to a country without an adequacy decision, they rely on the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum. A coach you join sees your data from wherever they are; a coach outside the European Union and the United Kingdom who coaches you there is bound by the standard contractual clauses that form part of the terms.

Changes to this policy

The version and the date at the top show which policy applies. Before a material change takes effect, we tell you in the app and ask you to accept the new policy before you continue.
