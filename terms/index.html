Coaches and athletes

Either side can end the coaching at any time from the app. When it ends, the coach keeps a read-only archive of the sessions logged while they coached, and the athlete keeps everything they logged; whether the athlete keeps a copy of their current programme is the coach’s setting, shown before the end.
